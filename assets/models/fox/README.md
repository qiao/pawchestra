# Fox model

This folder holds the source mesh of the fox, a copy with rebuilt arms and its weights, and the
rigged copy. The red fox and the tan fox share the rig. The app does not serve these files. It
loads the module `src/models/studio/fox.ts`, which this chain writes:

```text
fox.glb                            source mesh
   │  tools/blender/fox_arms.py    rebuilt arms and painted weights
   v
fox-arms.glb + fox-weights.json    `source` and `paintedWeights` of tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json
   │  pnpm rig-mesh fox
   v
fox.rig.glb                        rigged mesh
   │  pnpm studio-body fox
   v
src/models/studio/fox.ts           studio body of the app
```

`docs/agents/rigging.md` gives the steps of the chain.

Facts of the mesh:

- A weld at 1e-5 gives 1,559 vertices in one connected part. The mesh has 5 open edges and 2
  edges with more than two faces.
- The box is x -0.550 to 0.549, y -0.952 to 0.951, and z -0.545 to 0.536. Thus the fox is 1.903
  units high from the soles to the tips of the ears.
- The fox faces +z, with its left side at +x. These are the axes of the studio.
- The fox stands in an A-pose: the arms go down and out at about 48° below the horizontal.
- The eyes are brown and black faces of the head surface, with no shells. The tail hangs down
  behind the legs to 0.01 units above the soles.

The inner side of the upper arm joins the trunk down to the armpit. Thus the script follows the
route of `cat_arms.py`: a cut along the seam where the arm meets the trunk, a side wall of the
trunk under the arm, and a tube with a flat inner side on the wall. The arms are 1.21 times longer
from the socket, because a rig with arm segments does not stretch the arms.

The faces of the paws, the trunk, the legs, and the head are the faces of the source, with two
exceptions. The short edges of the thin faces at the cut loops collapse. Smooth Vertices smooths
18 vertices of the small notches at the back of the neck, below the ruff
(`step_smooth_notches()`). A run gives the same bytes:

```bash
blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/blender/fox_arms.py
shasum -a 256 assets/models/fox/*.glb assets/models/fox/fox-weights.json
```

The rig gives the faces near each shoulder joint and each elbow joint the normals of the skin with
no crease angle (`rootNormals` of the config). Each hold bends the elbows, and a crease angle
splits the normals of each inner elbow into a visible notch. The rig binds the arms straight, with
a supinated bind paw on each side (the role-neutral bind of design 4.4).

### tripo-p2/fox-paws.fbx

**Four-digit paws.** Each front paw of the source has three long digits that spread 47° apart, as
a hand. `tools/blender/fox_paws.py` (headless Blender 5.2) removes each front paw past the closed
wrist ring of 11 vertices at x 0.2765 (275 vertices on each side) and builds a new paw of quad faces
on the ring. The rest of the mesh does not change. The right paw is the mirror of the left paw.

- **Frame.** The middle of the wrist ring, the paw axis a = (0.996, -0.009, -0.086) of Blender
  (out and a little down, as the source paw), the up axis u, and the width axis w (forward).
- **Wrist.** A band of 11 quads and 3 triangles joins the wrist ring to the first palm ring of 14
  vertices (an alignment of the two rings by angle). A closed quad surface on a ring of 11 edges
  must hold an odd face, so the paw has these 3 triangles and no other.
- **Palm.** Four rings of 14 vertices (4 edges on the top, 4 on the bottom, 3 on each side) on a
  super ellipse (exponent 2.4), at 0.009, 0.021, 0.034, and 0.048 units past the wrist ring, with
  the half widths 0.029, 0.034, 0.0368, and 0.0372. The top gets lower to the front (half heights
  0.020 to 0.0098), and the bottom bulges into a palm pad (0.0205 to 0.0215, then 0.016).
- **Digits.** The front palm ring and 6 inner vertices (0.004 units back, the web) are the bases of
  four tubes of 8 vertices: the 4 columns of the palm. Each digit has an ellipse section of 0.0186 ×
  0.0196 units, rings at 0.006 units and at 1, 0.55, and 0.2 tip radii before the tip, and a round
  tip of 4 quads about a tip vertex. The middle digits are 0.031 units long past the palm, the outer
  digits 0.027. They spread 1° and 3° from the paw axis, so they lie close together, and each digit
  curls down by 2 × 5.5 t (19° at the tip of a middle digit).
- **UVs.** Each new face takes the box projection of its main normal onto one of four squares of
  even dark paw fur in the color map (UV (0.6797, 0.8730) on the top, (0.5493, 0.8755) on the
  bottom, (0.9194, 0.3032) on the sides, and (0.5654, 0.1719) on the front), at 0.66 UV for each
  unit. The source paw has 0.86 UV for each unit and the same dark brown (sRGB about 70, 40, 24).

Each side has 200 new faces, and the mesh has 3,077 vertices and 3,505 faces (2,635 quads and 870
triangles). The open edges and the edges of more than two faces are those of the source, and each
edge of two faces has one winding. The FBX holds the mesh in the studio units with the UV map, smooth
faces, and no material. A run gives the same mesh. The bytes differ only by the time stamp of the
FBX header.

```text
uv run --no-project --with numpy==2.5.4 --with pillow==13.0.0 python -I tools/fox-maps.py
```

The red map is the source scaled to 2048 pixels (Lanczos), WebP quality 60, method 6 (Pillow
13.0.0). No texel changes its color: the fur, the socks, the brows, and the eye paint stay. The
only change is the gaze shift of `tools/gaze_shift.py`. The source paints each iris at the inner
side of its eye opening, and with the source place the foxes looked to the side and down in the
play view on a phone and from the default camera of the hall. The shift moves each iris, with its
pupil, as one decal on the ball. Each moved texel is a copy of a texel of the same ball, and no
texel gets darker:

- The target of each iris is the middle of the eye opening in a view 30 degrees above the forward
  axis of the head of the seated bind (`VIEW_DOWN`), turned 4 degrees out (`GAZE_OUT`). The
  default camera looks down 29 degrees at the stage, and the head of a seated player turns down
  in the play.
- The left iris moves 18.5 degrees and the right iris 10.8 degrees on the ball. The two irises of
  the source are not at the same place.

**Tan color map.** The tan map is the red map with one Oklab step of the orange fur only
(`TAN_STEP`): the lightness +0.06 and the hue +18 degrees, with no chroma step. A texel that
leaves the sRGB gamut takes the largest chroma in the gamut at its new lightness and hue. The
share of the step of a texel falls to 0 for a chroma below 0.09, a lightness above 0.82, or a hue
more than 25 degrees from 50 degrees, so the cream, the dark socks, the lines, and the white keep
the colors of the source. The eyeballs keep their colors. The texels of the fur (54 % of the map)
go from the median Oklab L 0.635, C 0.163, h 49.7 to L 0.695, C 0.150, h 67.7. Each fur texel
keeps its step from the other fur texels, so the strands stay.

**Differences from the source.** The app light is warm and comes from above, so the orange fur
reads lighter and warmer than in the even gray light of an FBX viewer. The nose is a gloss face
group of one dark color with flat normals, so it shows facets. The irises lie nearer the middle of
the eye openings than in the source. Each eye has a blink lid.

### History

The first Tripo P2 fox (FBX SHA-256 `db116e3197e4f68838268a7a9c2def02427863545d6692c4435f5aaec4ed0845`)
took 12 rounds of edits in a live Blender. In the hall view it read with a worried face, muddy fur,
thin arms, a small head, and too large paws and eyes. The second Tripo P2 fox (FBX SHA-256
`598c8e8d989d30c04548aa0b0da8967cd44311c3d69fe1b59fc3acf7265cd795`, Tripo files of `f033bd5d`) had a
hand rig in headless Blender with a head 1.17 times larger and legs 1.88 times longer. A Tripo CLI
fox with small dark bead eyes (task `10130f2a-0bd4-4acd-bd30-5d7d03c3a482`) was a trial, and it is
dropped: the fox keeps realistic eyes. The hand rig of this mesh at c6f979952 had point edits of
the look (a muzzle 0.9 times as long, rounder cheeks, smaller paws, thicker arms, a thicker trunk,
thinner shins 1.07 times longer, a Taubin relax, and a belly key), a crease angle of 55, and color
maps with a shift of the fur and a round, larger iris. The old files and the steps of each edit are
in the git history of this folder.
